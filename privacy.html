TwoPaws Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TwoPaws is a private date-planning application designed to help users organise and share date plans and activities.

1. Information We Access

TwoPaws uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users. With the permissions granted by users, the application accesses Google Drive to store, retrieve and synchronise TwoPaws application data.

This data may include date plans, activities and related application information created by users.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to provide the application's date-planning, storage and synchronisation features. Firebase Realtime Database may be used to maintain lightweight synchronisation information between devices.

3. Data Storage

Application data may be stored locally on users' devices and in Google Drive. Synchronisation information may be stored using Firebase services.

4. Data Sharing

TwoPaws does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Data is processed through Google and Firebase services as necessary to provide the application's features.

5. Data Security

TwoPaws uses authentication and access controls intended to protect application data. Users should keep their Google accounts secure and only grant access to trusted devices.

6. Revoking Access

Users can revoke TwoPaws' access to their Google Account through their Google Account security settings. Removing access may prevent Google Drive synchronisation from working.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as TwoPaws develops. The latest version will always be available at this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the TwoPaws GitHub repository .
